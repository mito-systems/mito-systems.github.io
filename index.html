Introducing Ranger

Ranger is our groundbreaking platform that enables global citizen science. It empowers researchers and citizens to collaborate without traditional institutional barriers.

The publicly available Ranger Portal is available to all users; wildlife researchers are invited to create custom portals to address their specific research objectives.
